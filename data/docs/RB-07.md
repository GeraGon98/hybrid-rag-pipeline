# RB-07 · Inventory Desynchronization Between WMS and ERP

## Problem Description

The available inventory in ERP Orion does not match the stock levels in WMS Atlas. This is a known issue registered as T-1042 and typically appears after month-end closures with high volume.

## Known Root Cause

The INV-SYNC interface job stops processing when it receives an SKU with more than 18 characters and stops processing subsequent movements without marking an error.

## Resolution

Identify the problematic SKU in the INV-SYNC log, correct it in the item master, and restart the job with the --desde-ultimo-ok option. Then run the reconciliation report CONC-02 to confirm that the differences are zero.

## Prevention

Since March 2026, the item master validates the SKU length. The ticket T-1042 remains open until the vendor releases the definitive patch.
