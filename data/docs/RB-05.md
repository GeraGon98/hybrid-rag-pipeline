# RB-05 · Compromised Account or Phishing

## Indicators

A user reports a suspicious email in which they entered their password, or the email system detects login attempts from another country.

## Immediate Containment

Disable the account, revoke all active sessions, and enforce password and second factor reset. Block the malicious email sender and domain in the filter.

## Investigation

Review forwarding rules created in the mailbox, access logs from the last 14 days, and shared file downloads. Preserve evidence in the incident folder.

## Notification

If there was access to information classified as Confidential according to POL-08, the security officer notifies the address within a maximum of 24 hours.
