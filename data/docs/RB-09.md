# RB-09 · Full Disk on Application Servers

## Thresholds

An alert is triggered at 80% disk usage and is considered critical at 90%.

## Safe Cleanup

Compress and move logs older than 7 days from /var/log/apps to cold storage. Never delete files from /data/spool as they contain pending transactions to be sent.

## Expansion

If disk usage remains above 80% after cleanup, request volume expansion from the infrastructure team using a standard change process.
