# RB-12 · Onboarding Access for New Staff

## Request

Human Resources sends the ALTA-01 form at least 3 business days in advance of the start date.

## Base Access

Every employee receives email, VPN, and read-only access to the documentation portal. Role-based Orion ERP access requires approval from the employee's manager.

## Delivery

The service desk delivers credentials in person or via call, never by email. The user must activate MFA on the first day according to POL-02.
