# RB-03 · WMS Atlas Restart

## When to Apply

Use this procedure when dockside radio-frequency terminals cannot confirm picks or Atlas WMS takes more than 10 seconds to respond to a scan.

## Procedure

Notify shift supervisors to pause picking. Stop the atlas-core service, clear the message queue with purge_queue.sh, and restart it. A full restart takes approximately 7 minutes.

## Validation

Confirm that the pending task queue returns to zero and perform a test scan at platform 1. Record the start and end times in the ticket.
