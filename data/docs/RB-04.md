# RB-04 · Invoice Database Restoration

## Scope

Applies to the PostgreSQL database db-fact-01 that stores electronic invoices and credit notes.

## Available Backups

There is a full daily backup at 02:00 and WAL archiving every 5 minutes in CloudVault (SLA-I). This allows recovery to a point-in-time with a maximum data loss of 5 minutes.

## Restoration Procedure

Restore the latest full backup to a clean server, apply the WALs up to the desired time using recovery_target_time, and validate invoice counts against the SAT portal before redirecting the application.

## Authorization

All production restorations require approval from the finance leader and an emergency change registered according to POL-03.
